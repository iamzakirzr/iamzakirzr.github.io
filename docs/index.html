about

What I actually do

I'm a Senior QA Engineer at IQVIA, working on healthcare and life sciences platforms where "it looked fine when I clicked it" is not an acceptable standard of evidence. Most of my career has gone into building test automation frameworks that other engineers can actually live in — Playwright, Selenium, AccelQ, Python and C#, wired into Azure DevOps so the regression suite runs itself every day instead of the night before a release.

The newer half of the job is the interesting one. Enterprise chatbots, RAG pipelines and agentic workflows don't fail like deterministic software does: they fail quietly, fluently, and only sometimes. So I build evaluation frameworks instead of assertions — golden datasets, scored metrics, thresholds that a pipeline can fail on — and treat a drop in faithfulness the same way I'd treat a red test.

I also lead a team of five QA engineers and own the STLC across Scrum, Kanban and Waterfall delivery models, which mostly means translating between what a test report says and what a release manager needs to hear.

LLM & RAG evaluation

Golden datasets first, then scored runs — faithfulness, context precision, answer relevance, hallucination rate. DeepEval and RAGAS sit in the loop so a drop in response quality surfaces as a failing check, not as a support ticket three weeks later.

Agentic workflow validation

Multi-step agents get judged on what they did, not just what they said: tool-calling accuracy, reasoning traces, autonomous task execution, and whether context is still intact by step six.
